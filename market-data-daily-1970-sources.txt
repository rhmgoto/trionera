market-data-daily-1970.csv
Columns: date, nikkei, dow, usdjpy, btc.
Rows are calendar days from 1970-01-01 to 2026-05-29.
Weekend/holiday/missing dates are forward-filled from the latest available observation per series.
nikkei: Yahoo Finance chart API, ^N225 close.
dow: fja05680/dow-sp500-100-years DJA.csv close through 1991-12-31; Yahoo Finance chart API ^DJI close from 1992-01-01 onward.
usdjpy: FRED DEXJPUS. Values begin 1971-01-04; 1970 rows are blank until first observation.
btc: Yahoo Finance chart API, BTC-USD close. Values begin 2014-09-17; earlier rows are blank.
